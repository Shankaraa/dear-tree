# Code of Conduct

Dear Tree is built by people from very different backgrounds: students, professionals, activists, first-time contributors. We want everyone to feel safe doing their best work here.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Make room for beginners. Everyone started somewhere.
- Respect different languages, cultures, and levels of experience.
- Be honest, especially about data. Never inflate tree or survival numbers.

## What's not okay

- Harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind
- Sexual language or imagery
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Repeatedly derailing discussions or ignoring maintainers' requests

## Enforcement

If something happens that makes you uncomfortable, report it privately to the maintainers using [GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/Shankaraa/dear-tree/security/advisories/new). Only the maintainers can see it. Reports will be handled quietly and fairly, and we will not share your name without your say-so.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban contributors who break this code.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
