# UPI concept note (draft)

> **Status: an idea, not an implementation.** The prototype only has a mock screen. No payment code exists, and nothing is charged. This note is here so contributors can poke holes in it.

## Why UPI

Most people in India already pay with UPI. A tree dedication should be as easy as paying a friend, with no cards and no new wallet.

## Three ideas

1. **Pay once by UPI.** Support a sapling with a single payment.
2. **Monthly care by UPI AutoPay.** Instead of one big upfront payment, a small monthly mandate funds the steward for 24 months, and a payment is released only while the tree is confirmed alive. This ties money to survival, which is the point of the project.
3. **A UPI QR on the tag.** The permanent tag could carry a QR that opens the tree's page and lets a visitor chip in for care, such as replacing a sapling.

## Open questions

- **Who holds the money,** and under what legal structure (trust, Section 8 company, partner NGO)? Dear Tree should not hold funds casually.
- **How is a payment released?** What counts as "alive"? A steward photo, a second person's check, or both? What stops a steward from reporting a dead tree as alive?
- **Mandate mechanics.** What are the limits and rules for recurring UPI mandates, and does a survival-conditional release fit them?
- **Refunds and failures.** What happens to the money if a tree dies and cannot be replanted?
- **Compliance.** Tax treatment, receipts, and CSR rules for company payers.
- **Fraud and trust.** Fake tags, fake QR stickers, and fake collection pages.

If you have worked on payments, mandates, nonprofit finance or compliance, we would value your view. Open an issue or a discussion.

## Not goals

No tokens, no NFTs, no crypto payments. Keep it plain and trustworthy.
